PostScan Mail pricing, every fee explained

PostScan Mail's Starter plan is advertised "as low as $10" a month, but that's the price at its cheapest addresses. The same plan costs up to $45 at its most expensive ones, and opening a letter costs $2 on top. Here is the full price list as PostScan published it on November 22, 2026.

The three personal and small-business plans

PostScan sells the same three plans everywhere, but the price depends on which address (location tier) you pick. The lowest and highest prices on the pricing page are below [source].

All from PostScan Mail's pricing page [source]. "Mail items" means letters or packages. Free open-and-scans cover up to 10 pages each, then $0.25 per extra page. Annual billing is advertised as "2 months free," and on this page it also adds recipients and free scans.

Which price will you pay? PostScan shows the price after you pick an address. Use the cheapest tier's numbers ($10 / $20 / $30) only if your chosen address is in that tier. Our comparison table uses the lowest advertised price for every provider, so it shows the $10 figure.

The storage numbers differ by page. The pricing page says mail is free for 14 days on monthly plans (30 on annual). The FAQ says "the first 30 days of mail storage are free" and packages get 7 days, without mentioning the billing period [FAQ]. If you're on monthly billing, plan around the shorter window.

Junk mail counts toward your limit

"Yes, junk mail counts towards your account limit," PostScan's FAQ says, adding that some addresses may filter out "Presorted Standard" mail [FAQ]. That matters on Starter: 30 items includes catalogs and credit card offers. Traveling Mailbox and Virtual Post Mail both leave junk out of the count (see Traveling Mailbox pricing and Virtual Post Mail pricing).

Package lockers (no mail scanning)

  • Basic Locker, $4.95/month: $3 processing per item, $2 forwarding per item, packages stored free for 7 days, then $0.10 per pound per day [source].
  • Premium Locker, $9.95/month: $2 processing per item, $1 forwarding per item, 14 days free storage, then $0.10 per pound per day [source].

Signing up, Form 1583 and canceling

  • Notary: you fill out and notarize USPS Form 1583 and provide two IDs. PostScan's online notary partners charge $20 [FAQ]. Compare no-notary providers on our Form 1583 page.
  • Each extra name needs its own Form 1583, and the $5/month fee applies [FAQ].
  • Canceling: close the account under "Subscription & Billing." Stored mail is discarded, received mail is held for 6 months "per CMRA regulations," and after that new mail is returned to sender [FAQ].
  • USPS change of address can't redirect mail away from a CMRA, so you'll need to tell senders yourself when you leave [FAQ].

Who PostScan Mail fits

People who want a specific city address with free local pickup [source], who mostly look at envelope images, and who don't get much junk mail. If you want most letters opened, the $2 scans add up fast. See Traveling Mailbox vs PostScan Mail for a side-by-side.
