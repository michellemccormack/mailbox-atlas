Form 1583 rejected: reasons and fixes

Short answer: Most rejections come down to a mismatch or an unreadable document. USPS rules say that if the information on the form doesn't match the ID, the mail center must deny the application. Fix the specific item (name, home address, expired or blurry ID, wrong document type), upload again, and re-sign if the form changed. Re-review takes 1 to 3 business days at iPostal1 and about 2 to 3 at US Global Mail.

Your virtual mailbox can't accept mail until Form 1583 is approved, and some providers bill you whether or not it's approved. Below are the reasons USPS rules and providers' help pages give for rejections, with the fix for each, checked November 24, 2026.

Provider-specific rules that catch people

  • Earth Class Mail (LegalZoom): both IDs must be valid, not expired, and "in a language that uses the Latin alphabet," and the secondary ID must show your name "exactly as listed on the primary ID" [source].
  • PostScan Mail: documents must be "in English or English-translated." Foreign driver's licenses, national ID cards and foreign university IDs aren't accepted [source].
  • US Global Mail: a document it accepted when you signed up can be flagged now, because the 2024 USPS update "revised the list of document types accepted as secondary ID." Your secondary ID also can't be the same document type as your primary [source].
  • Stable: if documents are rejected (Stable's examples are poor image quality, expired IDs and mismatched information), the signature packet is deleted. You upload new documents and sign again [source].
  • iPostal1: forms are reviewed by its 1583 team, then sent to the mail center for final approval, which takes 1 to 3 business days [source].

How do I resubmit?

  1. Read the rejection email. US Global Mail emails the reason and reopens the upload step [source], and reviews new documents in about 2 to 3 business days [source].
  2. Fix only what was flagged. If the problem is wrong information on a pre-filled form, US Global Mail says "Do not sign a Form 1583 that contains incorrect information"; ask for a corrected form instead [source].
  3. Re-sign if the form changed. Stable asks you to sign a new signature packet [source]. US Global Mail regenerates the form and sends a new e-signature request, at no charge [source].
  4. If a notary is required again, iPostal1's online notary is $25 and PostScan Mail's partner notary is $20 [source] [source]. Providers that verify you on video without a notary are compared in Form 1583 and online notarization.

Does a rejection pause billing or deadlines?

  • Billing: iPostal1 says the charge "will process and auto-renew whether or not you choose to complete the Form 1583" [source].
  • Deadlines: at US Global Mail, your 30-day window keeps running while a correction is in progress [source]. If required documents aren't approved by the deadline, mail receipt is suspended until the account is compliant [source].
  • Mail in the meantime: a mail center with no Form 1583 on file for you must return your mail to the Post Office [DMM 508.1.8]. Hold off on your change of address until you're approved.

Living outside the U.S.?

A foreign passport works as the photo ID, but a non-U.S. driver's license doesn't. For the address ID, US Global Mail says documents showing your foreign home address (a lease, mortgage, vehicle registration, insurance policy or voter card) are "generally workable" [source].
