Monthly vs annual virtual mailbox billing

Short answer: A yearly plan usually costs about ten months' worth of the monthly price, so it pays off only if you keep the mailbox at least 10 or 11 months. Before you prepay, check the refund rule: US Global Mail gives no refund for unused time after 30 days [USGM], Stable gives none after 30 days from the first invoice [Stable], while Traveling Mailbox prorates annual refunds monthly [TMB].

Most providers advertise "2 months free" or a percentage off for paying a year up front. Here's the dollar saving at each provider that publishes both prices, how many months you need to stay to come out ahead, and what happens if you cancel early. Prices from each provider's own page, checked November 27, 2026.

"Annual wins if you stay" is the first month at which the monthly bills add up to at least the annual price, e.g. 9 months of USGM Scale is $585 and 10 is $650, against $588 up front. iPostal1 prices are for Standard locations; Select, Premium and Prestige addresses cost more [source]. US Global Mail prices are marked "*varies by address" [source]. PostScan's prices rise with the address tier, up to $45, $55 and $65 a month [source]. Traveling Mailbox and PhysicalAddress.com describe the annual discount but don't print an annual dollar price on their pricing pages; Virtual Post Mail lists monthly prices only.

Annual plans that include more, not just less

  • PostScan Mail gives annual plans more: Starter 2 recipients instead of 1, Standard 10 free open-and-scans and 3 recipients instead of 5 and 2, Premium 20 and 6 instead of 10 and 4 [source]. Free letter storage goes from 14 to 30 days and free package storage from 3 to 7 days [source].
  • Stable shows "Save 16%" on yearly billing [source], and offers 50% off Grow for businesses under $1 million in annual revenue or those using a Stable address to incorporate [source].

Which to choose

  1. Start monthly if you're not sure of your mail volume. Traveling Mailbox, for one, lets you "Change Plans Anytime" [source], and a wrong-sized plan can cost more in overage fees than the annual discount saves. Size it first with the virtual mailbox chooser.
  2. Switch to annual after a month or two once you know the plan fits. USGM says you can "start off with the Monthly plan and upgrade at any time" [source].
  3. Prepay right away only if you're sure you'll keep the address for a year, for example an LLC address already on state filings. See virtual mailbox for an LLC.
