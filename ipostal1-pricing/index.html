iPostal1 pricing, explained

The $9.99 iPostal1 plan is real, but it gets you 30 envelope images a month at a Standard address, for personal mail only. Opening a letter costs $2.25, and a business name means the $14.99 plan. Below is every published tier and fee, copied from iPostal1's pricing pages on November 20, 2026.

The three plan families

iPostal1 sells three kinds of plans. Each has four tiers that differ only by how many mail items you get a month [VMA] [VBA] [VO].

The yearly price works out to ten months of the monthly price. iPostal1 calls it "2 Months Free" [source]. All three families have no contract, and you can change plans every month [source].

Personal vs business: the $5 difference that matters

  • Virtual Mailing Address plans are "for personal use only." They include up to 6 recipient names but "no company name" [FAQ] [source].
  • Virtual Business Address plans add 1 company name (still up to 6 recipients) [source]. iPostal1 says to use these to "register your business" [source].
  • Virtual Office plans add a local or toll-free phone number and a fax number. Calls are metered: inbound calls to a local number cost $0.015 a minute, forwarding to a U.S. number costs $0.02 a minute, and voicemail beyond 250 free minutes costs $0.01 a minute [source].

If mail will arrive in an LLC's name, the real entry price is $14.99, not $9.99. See using a virtual mailbox for an LLC.

Free storage, then storage fees

  • Letters, letter-packs and packages under 12×12×12 in.: 30 days free. Packages up to 24×17×12 in.: 10 days. Bigger packages: 5 days [source].
  • After that, letters, large envelopes, magazines and postcards cost $1.10 per item per month. Packages cost $1.10 per pound (actual or dimensional weight, whichever is greater) per month, per 10 days or per 5 days, depending on size [source].

One-time costs

  • Form 1583: the online notary costs $25. You can also sign in person at many mail centers, and some charge "a small fee" for that. Customers living outside the U.S. must use the online notary [FAQ]. Why some other providers skip the notary entirely: Form 1583 rules.
  • Refunds: a full refund within the first 30 days "upon request, if account not used." Plans renew unless you cancel before the billing date [source].
  • Prices are "plus applicable taxes" [source].

What a typical month costs

Say you get 25 items a month at a Standard address, open 4 letters (under 10 pages each) and recycle the rest:

  • Personal 30-item plan: $9.99 + 4 × $2.25 = $18.99 [source].
  • Same usage with the 10-pack bundle ($1.80 per order): $9.99 + 4 × $1.80 = $17.19, once you've paid $18 up front [source].
  • Business 30-item plan: $14.99 + $9.00 = $23.99 [source].
  • First month: add $25 for the online notary, unless you sign in person [FAQ].

If you open most of your mail, a plan with scans included can cost less. Traveling Mailbox Basic is $15 with 35 page scans [source]. See the cheapest virtual mailbox and iPostal1 vs Anytime Mailbox.
