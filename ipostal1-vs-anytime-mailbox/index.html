iPostal1 vs Anytime Mailbox

These two have the biggest location networks, and both start around $9.99. The real difference is how they price. iPostal1 has one national fee schedule. At Anytime Mailbox, each mail center operator writes their own plans. So the answer to "which is cheaper" depends on the specific address you choose and how much mail you open. Checked November 20, 2026.

Worked examples

These use published prices only, at Standard iPostal1 addresses and the actual Anytime Mailbox locations named. Postage isn't included.

Light user: 12 letters a month, open 2 (3 pages each)

  • iPostal1 personal 30-item plan: $9.99 + 2 × $2.25 = $14.49 [source]
  • Anytime Mailbox, Arlington VA Bronze (15 items): $4.99 + 6 pages × $1.00 = $10.99 [source]
  • Anytime Mailbox, Omaha NE Bronze (30 items): $9.99 + 6 pages × $0.50 = $12.99 [source]

Heavier user: 28 letters a month, open 10 (3 pages each)

  • iPostal1 personal 30-item plan: $9.99 + 10 × $2.25 = $32.49. Prepaying with the 10-pack ($18) instead gives $27.99 [source].
  • Anytime Mailbox, Omaha Bronze: $9.99 + 30 pages × $0.50 = $24.99 [source]
  • Anytime Mailbox, Arlington: 28 items is over Bronze's 15. Silver is $14.99 for 60 items, + $10.00 for 10 letters at "$1.00 each" = $24.99 [source]

Both examples assume letters of 10 pages or fewer. Add the one-time Form 1583 cost in month one: $25 at iPostal1 if you use its online notary [FAQ].

Which should you pick?

Pick iPostal1 if…

you want the same fees wherever your address is, need several recipient names on a cheap plan, or want a registered agent and mailbox on one login.

Pick Anytime Mailbox if…

you'll compare a few locations' full plans. A good operator can be cheaper than iPostal1's $2.25 per scan, especially if you open a lot of short letters.

Pick neither if…

you'd rather skip the notary. Traveling Mailbox, Stable and US Global Mail do Form 1583 without one (details).
