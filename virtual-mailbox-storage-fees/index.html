How long virtual mailboxes keep your mail

A virtual mailbox stores the paper original of each letter and package for a set number of days. After that, it either starts charging storage or shreds the item. The scans stay online much longer. Here are the free periods and the fees after them, from each provider's own pages on November 23, 2026, with PhysicalAddress.com added on November 25, 2026.

What a forgotten box costs

A 5 lb package left 30 days past its free period:

  • Traveling Mailbox or PostScan Mail: 5 lb × $0.10 × 30 days = $15 [source] [source].
  • US Global Mail: $0.10 × 30 days = $3 today; from January 1, 2027, 5 lb × $0.10 × 30 = $15 [source].
  • LegalZoom Virtual Mail: 5 lb × $15 = $75 for the month [source].
  • iPostal1 (small box, monthly rate): 5 lb × $1.10 = $5.50 [source].
  • PhysicalAddress.com: $1 a week after the first free week, so 30 extra days is $4, and the fee is not by weight [source].

For letters, the difference is smaller but adds up if you keep originals: 20 letters held one extra month cost $20 at Traveling Mailbox ($1 each) [source], $22 at iPostal1 ($1.10 each) [source], and about $30 at US Global Mail or PostScan Mail ($0.05 × 20 × 30 days) [source] [source].

Scans last longer than paper

  • US Global Mail: scans kept 180 days on Scale, unlimited on Teams [source].
  • Traveling Mailbox: scans stay "until you delete them" [source].
  • Stable, LegalZoom, iPostal1, Virtual Post Mail, PhysicalAddress.com: unlimited digital storage [source] [source] [source] [source] [source]
  • Anytime Mailbox: scans are available "as long as your account remains active" [source].

When you cancel

USPS rules require a commercial mail receiving agency (the legal category for virtual mailboxes) to keep remailing mail for at least 6 months after your service ends, to the address on your Form 1583, with new postage. Neither you nor the provider files a USPS change-of-address order, and you can tell the provider in writing not to remail [DMM 508.1.8]. In practice:

  • PostScan Mail discards any stored mail when you close the account and holds newly received mail for 6 months, then returns it to the sender [source].
  • So before canceling, forward or download anything you want to keep, and update your address with senders. Every provider's policy is in how to cancel a virtual mailbox. More in living abroad and full-time RVers.

Avoiding storage fees

  • Shred or recycle what you've read: shredding is free at Traveling Mailbox, US Global Mail, LegalZoom and PhysicalAddress.com [source] [source] [source] [source]; PostScan Mail charges $1.00 per item for up to 10 pages [source].
  • Batch your forwarding before the free period runs out; see mail forwarding fees compared.
  • If you get a lot of packages, the free package period (3 to 60 days above) matters more than the plan price.
