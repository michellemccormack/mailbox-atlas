Before you pick an address

  • Form 1583. Renting a private mailbox at any of these mail centers means filing USPS Form 1583 with the mail center.[source] Whether you also need a notary, and what each provider charges for one, is on our Form 1583 page.
  • Using it for an LLC. Some states won’t accept a mailbox address as a registered agent or principal office address. See virtual mailbox for an LLC.
  • Banks. A mailbox usually works as a bank mailing address, but usually not as your home or business street address. See virtual mailbox for a bank account.
  • Fees after the monthly price. Forwarding, storage and check deposit fees vary by provider: forwarding, storage, check deposits.
