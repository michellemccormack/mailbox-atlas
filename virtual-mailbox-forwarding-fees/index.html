Mail forwarding fees compared

Every virtual mailbox charges postage when it ships your mail. What differs is the handling fee on top: some charge once per box, some charge for every item inside it, and a few charge nothing but postage. If you forward a stack of letters once a month, that structure matters more than the plan price. Fees below are from each provider's own pricing page, checked November 23, 2026, with PhysicalAddress.com added on November 25, 2026.

Ship one letter at a time instead and the order flips: PostScan Mail charges $2.00 per single-item shipment [source], the same as Traveling Mailbox [source], and LegalZoom charges $2.25 ($1.25 + $1) [source]. Per-item pricing gets expensive with big batches; per-shipment pricing rewards batching.

Ways to cut forwarding costs

  • Batch. With a per-shipment fee, one monthly box costs the same handling as one letter [source]. iPostal1 lets you schedule shipments weekly, bi-weekly or monthly [source].
  • Don't forward what you can scan. Most mail only needs to be read. Compare scan allowances in virtual mailbox pricing compared.
  • Watch storage clocks. Waiting to batch is free only until the free storage period ends; see how long virtual mailboxes keep your mail.
  • Checks are a separate service. Mailing a check to your bank has its own fee at most providers; see check deposit fees.

If you're away for months

For long trips, compare a virtual mailbox's forwarding against USPS Premium Forwarding Service, which packs up your mail at your Post Office and sends it weekly by Priority Mail for a $26.40 online enrollment fee plus $29.70 a week [USPS].
