Opus Virtual Offices vs Davinci Virtual

Both sell a business street address in an office building, with mail receipt and optional phone answering. The pricing models are opposite: Opus charges the same flat price at every location, while Davinci prices each building separately and adds a setup fee. Here's what each publishes, checked November 23, 2026.

First-year cost, Houston

  • Davinci, cheapest Houston address: $50 × 12 = $600, plus the setup fee (the FAQ says it starts at $150) [source] [source]. That's $750 or more, with no phone answering.
  • Opus address only: $59 × 12 = $708, no setup fee [source].
  • Opus all-inclusive: $99 × 12 = $1,188, including a live receptionist on weekdays, a business phone and fax number, and voicemail and fax to email [source].

If you only need an address, the cheapest Davinci building and Opus come out close in year one, and Davinci gets cheaper from year two because the setup fee is one-time. If you want calls answered, Opus's flat $99 is simpler to price; Davinci quotes receptionist service separately [source].

Opus add-ons

Opus lists these upgrades on its services page [source]: toll-free number, outbound calling app, texting, conference bridge, custom business email, registered agent and call recording at $10 a month each; extra extensions at $25 a month each; supervised transfer and message taking at $50 a month each. Receptionist hours are 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. weekdays (8 to 5 in the Pacific time zone) [source].

Do you need a virtual office at all?

Both of these cost several times what a virtual mailbox does. If you need a street address and scanned mail but not a receptionist or a lobby listing, a virtual mailbox starts under $10 a month (see virtual office vs virtual mailbox and the cheapest address by state). Neither Opus nor Davinci publishes mail scanning prices on the pages we checked; if you want mail opened and scanned, see virtual mailbox pricing compared.
