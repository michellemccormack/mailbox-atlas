Anytime Mailbox pricing: why it ranges so widely

Anytime Mailbox doesn't have one price list. Each location is run by an independent mail center operator who sets their own plans and per-item fees. So "from $9.99" can mean 15 or 100 items a month, and $0.18 or $1.00 per scanned page. On November 20, 2026 we read the "starting from" price of every U.S. location on Anytime Mailbox's state pages, and the full plans at five of them.

What Anytime Mailbox itself says

  • "Our cheapest virtual mailbox plans are $4.99. On average, our plans cost $9.99 per month. Some of our vanity addresses can cost up to $50" [source]
  • "All service plans show what the included services are for each plan before you sign up, and what the fees are for any additional actions you request beyond your chosen plan from your mail center operator" [source]
  • Each location page names its "Mail Center Operator" [source]

What stands out:

  • The $4.99 plan has the priciest scans. Opening one 3-page letter at Arlington costs $3.00. That's more than half the monthly fee [source].
  • "Just an Address" charges for every piece of mail. At Austin 9th St, 10 letters a month adds $14.40 to the $7.99 fee, for a total of $22.39. The $12.99 "Address Pro" plan at the same location includes 60 items and 60 scans [source].
  • Plan names aren't standard. "Bronze" means 15 items in Arlington and Astoria, but 30 in Omaha and DC [source] [source].
  • Yearly billing doesn't always save the same amount. Omaha's $109.99 a year compares with $119.88 for 12 monthly payments. Arlington's $56.99 compares with $59.88 [source] [source].

The yearly-only locations

Six U.S. locations show only a yearly price, so their "starting from" number looks very high next to monthly prices. Examples are Brooklyn (495 Henry St) and Beverly Hills (Santa Monica Blvd), both at $399.99, and Overland Park, KS at $319.99. At 495 Henry St, the single plan is "Gold" at $399.99 a year. It includes 240 items a month, $0.25 per scanned page, $2.00 per forward and 5 recipients [source]. That's about $33 a month, not $400.

Fees that aren't on the location card

  • Form 1583 notarization. Anytime Mailbox uses an online notary partner "for a nominal fee," or you can see your operator in person [source]. We couldn't find a published amount notary fee not published.
  • Overage, storage, shredding and check deposit are set per plan by the operator and shown under "Full Details" before sign-up [source]. They aren't on a central price list varies by location.

How to pick an Anytime Mailbox location without overpaying

  1. Estimate your mail: items a month, and how many you'll want opened.
  2. Compare the full plan at two or three nearby locations, not the "starting from" price.
  3. Multiply the per-page or per-item scan fee by your usual openings. That's where cheap plans get expensive.
  4. Check whether you need more than one recipient name, since many Bronze plans include just one [source].
