Virtual mailbox addresses on a driver's license

Short answer: Usually only as a mailing address. Federal REAL ID rules require "at least two documents ... that include the individual's name and principal residence" [6 CFR 37.11], and states ask for a residential address. Texas says "A business address is not acceptable" [37 TAC 15.25], and Florida asks for two documents with your Florida residential address [FLHSMV]. South Dakota is the main exception: full-time travelers can use a South Dakota mail-forwarding address with a residency affidavit [SD DPS].

Mailbox companies often say customers use their address at the DMV. iPostal1's FAQ says "the vast majority of states permit this, but the laws vary by state" [iPostal1], and US Global Mail says "Many of our customers have used their US Global Mail addresses for their driver's licenses" [USGM]. The states' own pages are stricter. Here is what the federal rule and three states with clear published rules say. Checked November 27, 2026. Rules change, so confirm with the licensing office before you go.

The federal baseline: REAL ID

  • Proof of address: "To document the address of principal residence, a person must present at least two documents of the State's choice that include the individual's name and principal residence" [6 CFR 37.11(f)].
  • What's printed on the card: the "Address of principal residence," except where state law or DMV procedure allows an alternative address, or for people in address confidentiality programs and a few other listed groups [6 CFR 37.17(f)].

So a mailbox address can only be printed where a state has chosen to allow an alternative address, and you still prove where you actually live.

Texas: why forwarded mail fails

Texas accepts bank, credit card and government statements as proof, but they must show your residential address [Texas DPS]. A statement sent to your mailbox shows the mailbox address, and anything re-addressed with a forwarding label is rejected outright [37 TAC 15.49(g)]. If you can't produce two documents, DPS says you "may be eligible to complete a Texas Residency Affidavit" [Texas DPS].

South Dakota: the full-timer route

South Dakota is the one state here that publishes a path for a PMB address, but only if you don't keep a home elsewhere: "if you maintain a residence in another state, you do not qualify as a full-time traveler" [SD DPS]. The affidavit also says a South Dakota license means jury duty notices from South Dakota [SD affidavit]. Our mail guide for full-time RVers covers which providers have South Dakota addresses and the open question of what counts as "virtual only."

What to do

  1. Keep your residence address on the license and use the mailbox for everything that only needs a mailing address. See changing your address to a virtual mailbox.
  2. For Form 1583, a driver's license is one of the accepted IDs; see Form 1583 and online notary.
  3. Full-time travelers who want South Dakota should read the affidavit wording before choosing a provider, and ask SD DPS if unsure.
