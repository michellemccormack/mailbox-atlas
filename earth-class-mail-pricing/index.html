Earth Class Mail pricing, now LegalZoom Virtual Mail

If you look up Earth Class Mail's prices today, you end up on LegalZoom. The plans start at $39 a month, but only 5 pieces of mail a month are included at that price, and every extra piece costs $3. Here is the full fee schedule as LegalZoom published it on November 21, 2026.

What changed: the brand and the web address

  • The pricing page moved. earthclassmail.com/pricing now sends you (HTTP 301) to LegalZoom's "LZ Virtual Mail" page [LegalZoom].
  • It's the same company. LegalZoom's page says, "Earth Class Mail is now part of LegalZoom" [source]. Help articles and the cancellation policy are still on help.earthclassmail.com [ECM help].
  • The prices haven't changed since our November 19 check. The plan prices and per-item fees on November 21 matched our November 19 comparison table ($39 / $59 / $119 MailBox plans) [source].
  • The address network: "53 real street addresses across 44 states," and you can't pick up mail in person. "Our service is entirely virtual" [source].

Signing up and canceling

  • Billing starts right away. "Billing starts upon account creation and is not dependent on the completion or approval of USPS Form 1583" [ECM help].
  • Form 1583: LegalZoom says, "If a notary is required, we make the process seamless with our free notarization service" [source]. If you don't have a Social Security number, you go through the notary session with a foreign passport, Matricula Consular or NEXUS card as your photo ID [source].
  • Canceling at the end of the billing cycle is free. A $50 fee applies only if you choose 6 more months of account access. During those 6 months, received mail is $3 per item, and shipments are $5 domestic or $10 international, plus postage and $1 per item [source].
  • Mail after you close: mail is shredded for 6 months, then returned to sender. Earth Class Mail says "USPS rules do not allow for mail forwarding" after you cancel. Reopening within 3 months costs $99. You can't pause an account [source].

Who Earth Class Mail fits

Teams that want company-run processing facilities, AI summaries and API integrations, and that get enough mail to justify Essentials or higher [source]. For a single person with a few letters a month, a $39 plan with a $3 overage is expensive next to the plans on our cheapest virtual mailbox page.
