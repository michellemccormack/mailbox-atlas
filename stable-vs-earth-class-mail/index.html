Stable vs Earth Class Mail for a startup

Both sell business-grade mail scanning to startups, but they charge in very different ways. Earth Class Mail (now LegalZoom Virtual Mail) has the lower sticker price and includes few pieces of mail. Stable costs more up front and includes six times as much. Which one is cheaper depends almost entirely on how much mail you get. Checked November 21, 2026.

With the 50% startup discount, Grow drops to about $29.50 on monthly billing, or $24.50 a month on yearly billing, for the first year. That assumes the discount applies to whichever billing interval you pick, which the page doesn't spell out [source] discount math is ours. At 12 or more real pieces a month, Earth Class Mail Starter costs more than Stable Grow at full price: $39 + 7 × $3 = $60 at 12 pieces, against $59 (at 11 pieces it's $57).

Which to pick

  • Very little mail (a handful of IRS and state letters): Earth Class Mail Starter at $39 is the cheaper of the two [source].
  • 15 or more pieces a month, or several company names on one box: Stable. Unlimited recipients and 30 included pieces make Grow cheaper [source].
  • You also need a registered agent: Stable lists it at $25/mo per state, alongside your mail in the same dashboard [source].
  • Paper checks: it depends on your volume. Earth Class Mail charges $10 per check without the subscription [source], while Stable's rate starts "as low as $3" [source]. See check deposit fees.
