iPostal1 vs Traveling Mailbox

Short answer: iPostal1 is cheaper if you mostly look at envelope images and open only a letter or two a month. Traveling Mailbox is cheaper once you open more than about two letters a month, because its $15 Basic plan includes 35 page scans, while iPostal1 charges $2.25 for each letter it opens. iPostal1 also has far more addresses to pick from and lets you collect mail in person. Traveling Mailbox skips the notary for Form 1583.

Both give you a street address, show you a picture of every envelope, and scan or forward on request. They charge for it in opposite ways: iPostal1 has a low monthly fee plus pay-per-use scans, and Traveling Mailbox has a higher fee with scans included. Here's the comparison from both companies' own pages, checked November 25, 2026.

Worked examples (Standard iPostal1 address, monthly billing)

Light use: 15 letters a month, 2 opened, nothing forwarded

  • iPostal1: $9.99 + 2 × $2.25 = $14.49 [source]
  • Traveling Mailbox Basic: $15, since 15 envelopes and a few pages fit in 40 / 35 [source]

Typical household: 25 letters, 10 opened (3 pages each), one forwarded batch

  • iPostal1: $9.99 + 10 × $2.25 = $32.49 plus shipping. With a prepaid 10-order bundle ($18), it's $27.99 plus shipping [source]
  • Traveling Mailbox Basic: $15 + $2 for the shipment = $17 plus postage. 25 envelopes and 30 pages fit inside 40 / 35 [source]

The break-even is low: at Standard addresses, three opened letters a month ($9.99 + 3 × $2.25 = $16.74) already cost more than Traveling Mailbox's $15. If you want a Select, Premium or Prestige iPostal1 address, add the surcharge before you compare [FAQ].

Pick iPostal1 if

  • You want a specific city or neighborhood. iPostal1 lists 4,250+ addresses [FAQ].
  • You mostly need to see who wrote and rarely need letters opened.
  • You live near the mail center and want to collect mail in person [FAQ].
  • You receive several checks at once: $4.95 covers up to 5 checks in one deposit, plus scans and shipping [source].

Pick Traveling Mailbox if

  • You read most of your mail as PDFs. 35 page scans a month are included on Basic [source].
  • You get a lot of junk mail. Marked junk doesn't count toward your limits [source].
  • You'd rather skip the notary. Traveling Mailbox handles Form 1583 by video call [source].
  • You want a business name without changing plans [source].
