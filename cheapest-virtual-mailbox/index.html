The cheapest virtual mailbox

Short answer: Anytime Mailbox advertises the lowest price, from $4.99 a month, but each location sets its own plans. PhysicalAddress.com Plan 30 starts at $7.98, PostScan Mail at $8.33 a month billed yearly, and iPostal1 at $9.99. If you open several letters a month, PhysicalAddress.com Plan 30 includes 10 content scans, and Traveling Mailbox Basic ($15 with 35 page scans and no notary) can still cost less overall once you outgrow those allowances. Cheapest for your situation

You just need an address

An Anytime Mailbox or iPostal1 location is often cheapest when you mostly recycle mail and only look at envelope images. PhysicalAddress.com Plan 30 at $7.98 is in the same range if you want one of its eight company-run addresses [source].

You open 5+ letters a month

PhysicalAddress.com Plan 30 includes 10 content scans in the $7.98 price [source]. Traveling Mailbox Basic includes 35 page scans for $15 [source]. At PostScan, five open-and-scans on Starter add $10 to the bill [source].

You want to skip the notary

Traveling Mailbox, Stable and US Global Mail handle Form 1583 without a notary [source] [source] [source]. That saves the $20–$25 online notary fee charged elsewhere [source] [source].

How much does Form 1583 add to the cost?

Some providers require a notarized USPS Form 1583 before they'll handle your mail. iPostal1's online notary costs $25 [source] and PostScan's partner notary costs $20 [source]. Over a year on a $9.99 plan, a $25 notary fee adds about 20%. See Form 1583 and online notarization.
