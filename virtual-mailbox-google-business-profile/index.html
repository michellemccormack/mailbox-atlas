Virtual mailbox addresses and Google Business Profile

Short answer: No. Google's guidelines say "P.O. boxes or mailboxes located at remote locations aren't acceptable," and "If your business rents a physical mailing address but doesn't operate out of that location, also known as a virtual office, that location isn't eligible for a Business Profile" [Google]. If you work from home or travel to customers, set up a service-area profile and hide your address instead [Google].

A virtual mailbox gives you a street address, so it's tempting to put it on Google Maps. Google's own rules close that door, and even the mailbox companies warn against it. Here is what the Business Profile guidelines say, what to do instead, and where a mailbox still fits. Checked November 27, 2026.

Google also says businesses that show an address "should maintain permanent fixed signage of their business name at the address" [source]. A mailbox number at a shipping store or mail-scanning facility doesn't meet that.

The mailbox companies say the same

Anytime Mailbox's FAQ: "If you plan to use a digital mailbox for GoogleMaps, GoogleAdSense, or for a Google Business profile, it's best not to order one, as Google may not recognize the address as valid" [Anytime Mailbox].

If you work from home or go to customers

Google's answer is a service-area business: "A business that visits or delivers to customers directly but doesn't serve customers at their business address," like cleaning services or plumbers [source].

  1. Hide your address. "If you're a service-area business, you should hide your business address from customers," for example a plumber working from home [source]. Google: "If you don't serve customers at your business address, remove your address from your Business Profile" [source].
  2. Set service areas by city or postal code, up to 20, and keep the overall area within about 2 hours' drive of your base [source].
  3. One profile only. Service-area businesses "can only have one profile for the whole area that they serve" [source].
  4. Age-restricted products need a storefront. Alcohol, cannabis and weapons businesses aren't allowed as service-area businesses without one [source].

Where a virtual mailbox still fits

  • On your website, invoices and business mail, so your home address isn't printed everywhere. See how to write a virtual mailbox address.
  • As your LLC's mailing address, separate from the registered office; see virtual mailbox for an LLC.
  • If you need a staffed address, a virtual office with real reception is a different product from a mailbox. It still has to meet Google's staffing rule above. Compare them in virtual office vs virtual mailbox and Opus vs Davinci.
