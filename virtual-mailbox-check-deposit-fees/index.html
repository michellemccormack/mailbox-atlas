Virtual mailbox check deposit fees, compared

If customers or clients still mail you paper checks, the deposit fee can cost more than the mailbox itself. Usually the provider endorses the check and mails it to your bank, and you pay a handling fee plus postage. Here's what each provider publishes, as of November 20, 2026.

Things to check before relying on mailed deposits

  • Will your bank take checks by mail? US Global Mail tells customers to call their local branch first and ask, "Do you accept checks by mail?" [source]
  • Mobile deposit from a scan depends on your bank's own rules. US Global Mail offers it as an option [source], but whether your bank accepts a deposit from a scanned image is up to the bank bank-specific.
  • The deposit fee usually comes on top of your plan. Earth Class Mail lists the check deposit subscription ($39/mo) separately from its MailBox plans, which start at $39/mo [source]. LegalZoom's FAQ now says check depositing "can be added for an additional fee" [source], so 5 checks a month on the Starter plan costs $78 a month in total (rechecked 2026-11-21).
