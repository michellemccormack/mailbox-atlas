Traveling Mailbox vs PostScan Mail

PostScan Mail is cheaper if you just want envelope images. Traveling Mailbox is cheaper as soon as you want letters opened, and it skips the notary. Here's every published fee from both, checked November 19, 2026.

Worked example

Take 25 letters a month, with 8 opened (about 3 pages each) and 5 shredded:

  • Traveling Mailbox Basic: $15. 25 envelopes and 24 pages are inside the allowance, and shredding is free [source].
  • PostScan Mail Starter: $10 + 8 × $2.00 for open-and-scan + 5 × $1.00 for shredding = $31. On Standard at $20 (5 free scans), it's $20 + 3 × $2.00 + $5 = $31 [source].

Add the $20 notary at PostScan in your first month [source].

When PostScan Mail wins

  • You want an address in a particular city and to pick up mail in person, which is free at most PostScan locations [source].
  • You rarely open mail and want the lowest monthly bill.
  • You need extras like HIPAA compliance ($25/mo billed annually) [source].

When Traveling Mailbox wins

  • You want most mail opened and scanned.
  • You'd rather skip the notary.
  • You forward in batches, since it's $2 per shipment no matter how many pieces are in it [source].
