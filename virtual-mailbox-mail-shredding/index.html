Mail shredding at virtual mailboxes: fees and policies

Short answer: Most providers shred for free. Traveling Mailbox, US Global Mail, Virtual Post Mail, Stable, LegalZoom Virtual Mail (Earth Class Mail) and PhysicalAddress.com all list free shredding. iPostal1 charges $2.25 per shred order, with cheaper bundles, and PostScan Mail charges $1 per item. Some providers also shred paper you haven't acted on once free storage ends, so check that rule before you rely on them to keep originals.

A virtual mailbox scans the outside of each envelope, and then you decide what happens to it: open and scan, forward, or destroy. If you plan to throw away most of your mail, the shredding fee and the auto-disposal rule matter more than the plan price. Here's what each provider publishes, checked November 28, 2026.

Shredding vs recycling vs discarding

Some providers offer two ways to get rid of mail. Anytime Mailbox says its operators "recycle all mail that is disposed of", and shredding is the option for "documents containing sensitive information" [source]. iPostal1 includes a free "discard mail items" service, while a shred order costs $2.25 [source]. PostScan Mail lets you choose recycle or shred for each item [source]. Anything with an account number, Social Security number or signature belongs in the shred pile.

Where the shredding happens

  • Traveling Mailbox: "Our team destroys the paper original at our processing facility" in Sanford, North Carolina [source] [KB].
  • Virtual Post Mail: mail is "shredded on-site using a mobile shredding service", so it "does not leave our facility for shredding" [source].
  • LegalZoom Virtual Mail: unwanted mail "is securely shredded and recycled on-site" [source].

What 20 shredded letters a month costs

Say 20 letters a month arrive that you never want opened, each under 10 pages:

  • Traveling Mailbox, US Global Mail, Virtual Post Mail, Stable, LegalZoom, PhysicalAddress.com: $0 in shred fees [TMB] [USGM] [VPM] [Stable] [LZ] [PA].
  • PostScan Mail: 20 × $1.00 = $20 [source].
  • iPostal1: 20 × $2.25 = $45 as single orders, or $30 with a 20-order bundle [source]. Using the free discard service instead costs nothing [source].

Shredded items still count as received mail on plans with a monthly item limit, unless the provider excludes junk. See does junk mail count?

Don't lose an original you need

  1. Know the auto-shred clock. At Traveling Mailbox and Stable, paper you leave alone is shredded after 30 days [TMB] [Stable]. Put a hold on, or forward, anything you need to keep, like a car title, a birth certificate or a new passport.
  2. Save scans before you close an account. At US Global Mail, mail that arrives after you close an old account "will be securely shredded" [source].
  3. Request shredding carefully. Traveling Mailbox advises saving any digital copies or originals you want "before choosing a disposal action" [source].
