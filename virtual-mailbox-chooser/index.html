How the estimate works

  • For each service we try every plan with published allowances and pick the cheapest for your numbers: plan price plus extra-item, extra-scan, check and extra-name fees. Monthly billing, standard address, before postage and sales tax.
  • Checks are assumed to go to your bank together in one shipment a month.
  • iPostal1, PostScan Mail and Anytime Mailbox prices are "from" prices: the mail center you pick can charge more. Anytime Mailbox lets each location set its own plans and fees, so we can only show its starting price.
  • Where a provider doesn't publish a fee (for example PostScan Mail's check deposit), the estimate says so and leaves it out.
  • Fee sources: price table, check deposit fees, forwarding fees and each provider page linked above.
