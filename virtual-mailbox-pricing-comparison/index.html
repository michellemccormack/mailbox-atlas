Virtual mailbox pricing, compared

Short answer: Entry plans start at $4.99 (Anytime Mailbox, varies by location), $7.98 (PhysicalAddress.com, in Boise), $9.99 (iPostal1), $10 (PostScan Mail) and $15 (Traveling Mailbox). Scans and forwarding decide the real bill: at 30 letters with 10 opened and scanned, PhysicalAddress.com Plan 30 stays at $7.98 and Traveling Mailbox Basic stays at $15, while PostScan Mail Starter comes to $30 or more. [source][source][source][source][source]

The sticker price is the smallest part of the bill. What decides the real cost is how many pieces of mail are included, what each scan past that costs, and what forwarding costs. Here are the published numbers for nine services, copied from each provider's own page on November 19, 2026. US Global Mail's entry plan was rechecked on November 24, 2026, and PhysicalAddress.com was added from its own pages on November 25, 2026.

How to read this: "from" prices vary by location for iPostal1, Anytime Mailbox and PostScan Mail, because independent mail centers set them. PhysicalAddress.com runs its own locations, and its starting price still varies by city. The table shows the lowest price each provider advertises. Annual billing is cheaper at several providers, and both prices are shown where the page lists both.

What does a virtual mailbox really cost per month?

Say you get 30 letters a month and want 10 of them opened and scanned (about 3 pages each), with nothing forwarded.

  • PhysicalAddress.com Plan 30: $7.98 at the price published on the pricing page (the same as its Boise starting price). 30 envelope scans and 10 content scans are included, so this example stays at the plan price [source].
  • Traveling Mailbox Basic: $15. 30 envelopes and 30 page scans fit inside the 40-envelope / 35-page allowance [source].
  • PostScan Mail Starter: from $10, plus $2.00 for each of the 10 open-and-scans ($20), so from $30. The Starter plan lists no free open-and-scans; Standard includes 5 [source].
  • Stable Grow: $59 monthly ($49 if paid yearly). 30 pieces is the monthly limit, and open-and-scan is free [source].
  • Earth Class Mail Starter: $39 covers 5 digitized items, so 30 items is well past the allowance. The overage rate wasn't clearly listed.

Someone who scans very little and mostly wants an address does best with a cheap Anytime Mailbox or iPostal1 location. Anyone who wants most of their mail opened pays less with a plan that includes page scans.

Is a virtual mailbox a street address or a P.O. Box?

Every provider above gives you a street address at a Commercial Mail Receiving Agency (CMRA), not a USPS P.O. Box. USPS requires every CMRA customer to sign Form 1583 before the CMRA can handle their mail [USPS]. Some banks and agencies won't accept a CMRA address. Anytime Mailbox, for example, says it doesn't recommend using its address as your official business address because "some states and institutions may have restrictions" [source].
