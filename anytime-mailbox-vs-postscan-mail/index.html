Anytime Mailbox vs PostScan Mail

Short answer: They're built differently. Anytime Mailbox is a marketplace: each address is run by an independent mail center that sets its own plans and fees, so you compare location by location. PostScan Mail sells the same Starter, Standard and Premium plans everywhere, with one fee list, and only the base price changes by address. If you open few letters and find a cheap Anytime location with fair per-page scan fees, Anytime can cost less. If you want predictable fees, PostScan is easier to price in advance.

Both offer street addresses in hundreds of cities, envelope scans, forwarding and in-person pickup at most locations. Here's how the pricing compares, using Anytime Mailbox's location pages and FAQ and PostScan Mail's pricing page and FAQ, checked November 25, 2026.

Worked example: 20 letters a month, 5 opened (3 pages each), one forwarded batch of 5

  • PostScan Starter (cheapest tier): $10 + 5 × $2.00 opens + $2.00 + 4 × $0.50 forwarding = $24 plus shipping [source]
  • PostScan Standard (cheapest tier): $20, with the 5 opens included, + $4 forwarding = $24 plus shipping [source]
  • Anytime Mailbox, Omaha Bronze: $9.99 + 15 pages × $0.50 + one $5.00 bundle = $22.49 plus shipping [source]
  • Anytime Mailbox, Austin "Just an Address": 20 × $1.44 = $28.80 for receiving alone, on top of the $7.99 fee. At that volume, the same location's $12.99 "Address Pro" plan (60 items and 60 open-and-scans included, $4.95 per forward) is the better buy [source]

At PostScan, a higher-tier address raises every line above by the same plan-price difference (up to $35 a month more on Starter) [source]. At Anytime Mailbox, the result depends entirely on the location you choose, so run your own numbers on two or three nearby ones.

Leaving either service

  • Anytime Mailbox: close the account under Settings. Mail that arrives afterward is stored by the operator for up to six months, "and storage fees may apply"; forwarding costs extra [source].
  • PostScan Mail: close it under Subscription & Billing. Stored mail is discarded, and mail received over the next 6 months is held, then returned to the sender [source].
