PhysicalAddress.com pricing

Short answer: The cheapest published plan is Plan 30 at $7.98 a month, with 30 envelope scans and 10 content scans. Additional scans are $0.20. Starting prices are higher in some cities: Las Vegas is $8.98, several others are $9.98, Laguna Beach is $10.98 and the Wall Street address is $19.98. Annual billing is advertised at 20% off, but the pricing page does not list the annual dollar amounts. [source][source]

PhysicalAddress.com runs its own mail locations instead of using third-party mail centers, and it says mail is scanned on site the same business day [source]. Every figure below is from its own pages, checked November 25, 2026.

Form 1583

You can start using the address right away, but PhysicalAddress.com cannot scan, forward or upload mail until USPS Form 1583 is completed [source] [source]. After you sign up, it emails a pre-filled form and a link to a partner online notary that "supports international clients." You can also sign with a notary or at one of its locations. It does not publish the online notary's fee [source].

A 30-letter example

If you receive 30 letters a month and open 10 of them, Plan 30's allowance (30 envelope scans and 10 content scans) covers that volume, so the monthly plan price is $7.98 at the Boise rate published on the pricing page, before any forwarding [source]. The same mail volume on Traveling Mailbox Basic is $15, and on PostScan Mail Starter it is from $10 plus $2.00 for each of the 10 open-and-scans [source] [source]. In New York the published starting price is $19.98, which already costs more than Traveling Mailbox Basic before scans are counted [source].

Canceling

Cancel by phone at (844) 888-6364, by emailing support@physicaladdress.com, or by opening a support ticket in your account. If Form 1583 was completed, it will keep accepting mail for 6 months after you cancel and will scan and upload each piece for free; forwarding in that period "will incur additional fees." There is a 30-day money-back guarantee [source] [source].
