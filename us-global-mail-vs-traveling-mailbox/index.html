US Global Mail vs Traveling Mailbox

Both scan your mail, forward it worldwide and skip the notary for Form 1583. The gap is price. Traveling Mailbox starts at $15 a month. US Global Mail's cheapest current plan is Occasional, at $25 a month or $21 billed annually. Prices are marked "*varies by address." Here's what that money gets you, using both companies' own pages on November 24, 2026.

Worked example: a typical household

25 letters a month, 10 opened (about 3 pages each, so 30 pages), one forwarded batch a month:

  • Traveling Mailbox Basic: $15 + $2 for the shipment = $17 + postage. 25 envelopes and 30 pages both fit inside 40 / 35 [source].
  • US Global Mail Occasional: $25 + 10 × $2 = $45 + shipping monthly, or $21 + $20 = $41 + shipping billed annually, because interior scans are $2 each [source]. Scale, at $65 + shipping monthly or $49 billed annually, includes those opens in its 360-a-year allowance [source] [2].

At this volume, Traveling Mailbox is still cheaper than Occasional, because Occasional charges $2 to open each letter. Scale includes the opens, but starts at $65 a month (or $49 billed annually). Add 3 paper checks a month and Traveling Mailbox goes up by 3 × $4.95 = $14.85 plus postage, while US Global Mail adds only shipping [source] [USGM]. Traveling Mailbox remains the lower bill in this example.

When US Global Mail is worth it

  • Several businesses or family members on one box. Scale includes 10 recipients, against 3 on Traveling Mailbox Basic and 5 on US Global Mail Occasional [source] [TMB].
  • You need HIPAA with a BAA, SOC 2, team permissions or an API [source].
  • You ship lots of packages internationally and want the consolidation and carrier discounts US Global Mail advertises [source]. Compare quotes. The discount claim is the company's own.

When Traveling Mailbox is the better buy

  • You mainly want letters scanned and the occasional batch forwarded. $15 covers most single households, and the company says most customers fit on Basic [source].
  • You want to keep scans forever without paying for a higher tier (unlimited cloud storage on every plan) [source].
  • You're military or a veteran (10% off) [source].
