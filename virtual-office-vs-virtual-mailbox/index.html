Virtual office vs virtual mailbox

Both give you a real street address that isn't your home. A virtual mailbox is built around mail: it scans envelopes, opens letters on request and forwards what you need. A virtual office is built around looking like an office: a business phone number, someone to answer it, a name in the building lobby, and rooms you can book. Here's what that difference costs, from the providers' own price lists on November 23, 2026.

Going from a basic virtual mailbox to a virtual office costs roughly $300 to $1,000 more a year. That money buys phone service and a more office-like address, not better mail handling: the mailbox plans above publish more detail on scans and forwarding than the virtual offices do.

Which one you need

  • Virtual mailbox: you need an address for mail, an LLC's mailing address, or to read mail while traveling. Start with the cheapest virtual mailbox or the cheapest address in your state.
  • Virtual office: clients call you, visit you, or look you up, and you want a receptionist or a room to meet in. Compare Opus vs Davinci.
  • Neither replaces a registered agent in states that require the agent to be physically present at the registered office; see virtual mailbox for an LLC.
  • Bank account? Banks have their own address rules; see can I use a virtual mailbox for my bank account?
