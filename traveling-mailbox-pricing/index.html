Traveling Mailbox pricing: which plan to pick

Traveling Mailbox has three flat-priced plans: $15, $25 and $55 a month. Unlike most providers, it doesn't charge per open-and-scan. Each plan includes a set number of page scans, and you pay small overage fees past that. Here's what each plan covers and the point where upgrading saves money, from the pricing page as of November 22, 2026.

Source: Traveling Mailbox pricing page [source]. All plans include free shredding, unlimited cloud storage, and plan changes at any time. Junk mail you mark as junk "won't count towards your plan limits" [source].

  • Envelope scans vs page scans: every envelope that arrives is scanned on the outside, and that doesn't use your page-scan allowance. Page scans are for opening and scanning the contents [source].
  • Rollover: the plan cards say "Your Scans Rollover" on all three plans, but the footnote limits rollover of unused envelope and page scans to the Extended, Small Business and Enterprise plans [source]. Count on rollover only if you're on Extended or higher.
  • Annual billing: the FAQ says there are no contracts. You can pay month to month, or "subscribe to an annual plan to get 2 months free," and annual plans "are refundable on a monthly schedule" [source].
  • Minors can be added to an account and don't count toward the recipient limit [source].

When to upgrade: the break-even points

Monthly cost = plan price + $0.25 per envelope over the allowance + $0.50 per page over the allowance [source]. Junk you mark as junk is excluded.

In the 150-envelope row Small Business ($55) is the cheapest. Rule of thumb: if you regularly go over Basic by more than about 13 envelopes and 13 pages, Extended costs less. Rollover on Extended (but per the footnote, not Basic) helps if your mail comes in uneven months [source].

Form 1583 without a notary

After you subscribe, you complete USPS Form 1583 inside your account, then "schedule an appointment with us to verify your form. A notary is not required" [source]. That saves the $20 to $25 online-notary fee charged at some other providers (see our Form 1583 guide).
