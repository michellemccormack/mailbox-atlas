A cheap address isn't a registered office

People often want a Delaware, Wyoming or Texas address because they're forming an LLC there. A virtual mailbox can be the company's mailing address, but each state sets its own rules for the registered office, and they ask for more than a mail drop:

  • Texas: the registered office "must be a physical address in Texas where the registered agent can be personally served with process during business hours. It cannot solely be the address of a mailbox service" [Texas SOS].
  • Wyoming: the registered office must be "a street address in Wyoming which shall be a physical location" where the registered agent, or a person with an agency relationship with the agent, "is physically present" to accept service [W.S. 17-28-101].
  • Delaware: every LLC must maintain a registered office in Delaware and a registered agent "having a business office identical with such registered office" [6 Del. C. §18-104].
  • Florida: the registered agent's business address must be "identical to the address of the registered office" [§605.0113], and the articles must give the agent's "street address in this state" [§605.0201].
  • California: the agent must be a California resident or a registered corporate agent, and the agent's "physical street address" is public record [CA SOS].

So the cheapest setup in these states is usually a registered agent plus a separate mailbox, or a mailbox plan that includes an agent. Virtual Post Mail has business addresses in Keller (TX), Brandon (FL), Claymont (DE) and Covina (CA), and includes a free registered agent in California, Delaware, Florida, Nevada and Texas on plans from $20 a month [source]. Before you pick the $5.99 address

  • Check the plan, not just the price. A low starting price often comes with a small mail allowance and per-scan fees. Compare what you'll actually open each month with the cheapest virtual mailbox guide.
  • Add the Form 1583 cost. Every provider needs USPS Form 1583 before it can handle your mail; some charge for the notary. See Form 1583 and online notarization.
  • Some providers publish one price list. Traveling Mailbox's pricing page shows a single $15 Basic plan with 35 page scans included [source], and most of its customers use its home base in Sanford, NC [source] (Traveling Mailbox pricing).
  • PhysicalAddress.com also has company-run addresses in several of these states, with starting prices of $9.98 in Dallas, Orlando and Wilmington, $10.98 in Laguna Beach and $19.98 on Wall Street, plus $7.98 in Boise and $8.98 in Las Vegas.
